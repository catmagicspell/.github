About

We are a PSP homebrew development team from Turkey. We are not profiting from anything. We are only doing this just because we enjoy it.

Cat Magic Spell got established in May 19, 2022 as a Work in Progress Turkish console hacking news & discussion Discord server. It got into its current state around February 2023. We are not limited to PSP, we are accepting every homebrew developer for any console. Refer to the Join Us page for more information.
