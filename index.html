About
We are a PSP homebrew development team hailing from Turkey. We are not profiting from anything. We are doing this just because we enjoy it.

Cat Magic Spell was established on May 19, 2022, as a work-in-progress Turkish console hacking news and discussion Discord server. It evolved into its current state around February 2023.
We are looking for fellow homebrew creators and welcome developers for any platform. Refer to the Join Us page for more information.
