About
We are a PSP homebrew development team hailing from Turkey. We are not profiting from anything. We are only doing this just because we enjoy it.

Cat Magic Spell got established in May 19, 2022 as a Work in Progress Turkish console hacking news & discussion Discord server. It got into its current state around February 2023.
We are looking for fellow homebrew creators and we are okay with any platform. Refer to the Join Us page for more information.
